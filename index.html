How it works

The system records whether your pre-defined condition was met. You remain in control at every step.

1

Set your alarm

Choose your reference location and target time. The app sets a 100m radius.

2

Set your commitment

Set the amount you’ll be charged if you don’t unlock in time. You’re in control of your commitment.

3

Move to unlock

To avoid the charge, go to the 100m+ location and tap the button before the time is up.

Requirements: An active internet connection and enabled location services are required to verify your movement.

FAQ

Does the app charge me automatically?

Any payment requires clear user confirmation in advance, based on the user’s own chosen settings. We do not apply hidden or unexpected charges.

How is location used?

Location is used only to verify you have moved 100m away from your starting point. See the Privacy Policy for details, storage, and deletion options.

What if location services fail?

If a technical issue may have impacted the recorded outcome, contact support for review. We handle disputes case-by-case per our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
